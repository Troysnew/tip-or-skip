The USPTO explains that a dead registration can still matter if the name remains in commercial use. Search both similar marks and related goods/services; class numbers alone do not resolve a conflict. An exact-name search is only an initial screen. See [USPTO search guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/federal-trademark-searching).

## Remaining name work

1. In the [USPTO search system](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/), run each query separately: `CM:"tip or skip"`, `CM:"tiporskip"`, `CM:"skip tip"`, `CM:"skiptip"`, and `CM:"skip the tip"`. Expand to close spellings and sound-alikes, rather than stopping at these examples. Review live results and any dead marks that may remain in use.
2. Review the full record for serial 85511792 in [TSDR](https://tsdr.uspto.gov/). Preserve status, owner, goods/services, and relevant dates in the research record.
3. Search each close name directly in Apple App Store and Google Play, plus general web results, company records, and social platforms. Capture links and dates for potentially related services.
4. Check desired domains with a registrar and desired handles on each platform. Domain or handle availability is separate from permission to use the brand.
5. Before committing substantial launch spending, have a US trademark attorney assess the old mark's use and the most relevant similar results. If launch markets expand, add the relevant countries' registers and use searches.

No domain, handle, business name, or trademark was registered or purchased as part of this work. The symbol and wordmark have not had a separate design-mark clearance search.
