Common questions

How do I restore Pro on a new phone?

Open the upgrade screen in the app and tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought Pro with.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. Pro is a single $2.99 payment.

Where do the tipping recommendations come from?

Each service links to the published guide or article it is based on, such as the Emily Post Institute. One service with no published guide is labeled as common practice. Survey numbers come from Pew Research Center (2023) and Bankrate (2025).

Are my receipt photos uploaded?

No. Receipts are read on your phone with its built-in text recognition. The photo is not sent anywhere.

The receipt scan got a line wrong.

You can edit any item or amount before splitting. If a receipt keeps reading wrong, email us a photo of it and we'll use it to improve the scanner.

Where do I change my Venmo, Cash App or PayPal name?

On the About tab, under How friends pay you. You can also edit them from the Request screen.

Does the Pay button send money for me?

No. It opens the pay app you choose and copies the amount so you can paste it. You finish the payment in that app.

How do I delete my data?

Everything is stored only on your phone. Deleting the app deletes it all.
